# Design System

## Visual foundation
- Primary color: navy #1B2A4A
- Accent color: blue #2F6FED
- Supporting background: soft blue #EAF1FE
- Text color: dark slate #101828
- Borders and dividers: light gray #E4E7EC

## Typography
- Font stack: Segoe UI, Inter, Arial, sans-serif
- Headings: bold and slightly condensed for clarity
- Body copy: comfortable line height and readable spacing

## Layout patterns
- Maximum content width: 1180px
- Generous section spacing for a calm and refined feel
- Grid-based layouts for cards, impact stats, and feature sections
- Mobile-first responsive behavior with stacked layouts below 600px

## Common components
- Header with logo and simple navigation
- Hero section with a strong headline and clear CTA
- Feature cards for services, programs, and trust signals
- Impact metrics with bold numerals and short labels
- Donation and volunteer forms with clear field labels
- Footer with navigation and organization references

## Content style
- Use concise, empathetic copy
- Lead with impact and community benefit
- Keep CTAs clear and action-oriented
- Avoid overly dense paragraphs

## Assets
- Use the existing image assets already present in the workspace where possible.
- Preserve the current trust and humanitarian tone in photography and illustrations.
