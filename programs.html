What we do

Hospital & community programs

Two arms of work: direct medical support inside the hospital, and preventive health programs out in the community.

Hospital support activities

Direct financial and clinical support for patients who can't afford treatment.

Free Surgeries

Fully sponsored surgical procedures for eligible patients who cannot afford life-saving operations.

→

Dialysis Support

Ongoing dialysis cost coverage and treatment support for kidney patients requiring regular care.

→

Cancer Treatment Assistance

Chemotherapy and treatment cost support for cancer patients undergoing intensive care.

→

Maternal & Child Health

Safe delivery, newborn care support, and maternal health programs for expecting mothers.

→

Subsidized Investigations

Reduced-cost diagnostic tests, scans, and medical investigations for eligible patients.

→

Emergency Medical Aid

Rapid-response funding for emergency cases requiring immediate medical intervention.

→

School Health Programs

Routine check-ups and health screenings for children in partner schools across Bihar.

→

Medical Camps

Free consultation and health screening camps held at the hospital and nearby communities.
