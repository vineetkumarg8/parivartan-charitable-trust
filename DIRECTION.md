# Impeccable Direction

## Project summary
Parivartan is a healthcare and social welfare website for a trust that supports medical aid, community outreach, volunteer engagement, and donations. The current site is a polished static HTML experience with a strong blue-and-navy brand system.

## Primary goals
- Preserve the existing trust-building visual identity.
- Keep the website fast, accessible, and responsive.
- Make the mission, programs, donation path, and volunteer opportunities clearer.
- Maintain a simple static HTML/CSS implementation without introducing unnecessary frameworks.

## Brand direction
- Name: Parivartan
- Tone: compassionate, credible, service-focused, and community-driven
- Visual feel: calm, trustworthy, modern, and professional

## Content priorities
1. Home: introduce the mission, highlight impact, and guide users to donate or get involved.
2. About: explain the organization’s purpose and values.
3. Programs: showcase healthcare and welfare initiatives with clear visuals and concise copy.
4. Donate: make the giving journey simple and reassuring.
5. Involve: present volunteering and community participation options.
6. Resources and Legal: provide helpful references and policy clarity.

## Design constraints
- Use the existing blue/navy palette and clean card-based layout.
- Keep the navigation consistent across all pages.
- Maintain high contrast for readability and accessible text sizing.
- Favor concise, human-centered copy over marketing-heavy language.
- Respect the existing imagery and content structure.

## Success criteria
- The site feels polished and trustworthy on desktop and mobile.
- Users can quickly understand what Parivartan does and how to help.
- The experience remains cohesive across the home, about, programs, donate, involve, resources, and legal pages.
