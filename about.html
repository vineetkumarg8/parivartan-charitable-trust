Parivartan

A registered charitable trust promoted and supported by Dr. Bimal Hospital, working to make quality healthcare reachable for patients who can't afford it.

Who we are

Parivartan is registered under the Indian Trusts Act and works in close partnership with Dr. Bimal Hospital, Patna, to extend medical support to patients who cannot afford treatment on their own.

Every donation is tracked from receipt to disbursement, and the Trust publishes annual reports and audited financials so donors and partners can verify exactly how funds are used.
