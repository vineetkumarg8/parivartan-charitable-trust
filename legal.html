Legal & compliance

Click any section to expand it.

Parivartan collects only the information needed to process donations, respond to enquiries, and issue tax receipts — name, contact details, and payment confirmation. We never sell or share personal data with third parties, and donor information is stored securely and retained only as long as required for legal and accounting purposes.
By using this website you agree to use it only for lawful purposes related to donating, volunteering, or learning about the Trust's work. Content on this site — including photographs, reports, and program descriptions — belongs to Asha Seva Trust and Dr. Bimal Hospital and may not be reproduced without permission.
Donations made online are generally non-refundable, as funds are often allocated quickly to active patient cases. If a donation was made in error — duplicate payment, incorrect amount — contact us within 7 days and we will review the request individually. Monthly recurring donations can be cancelled at any time from your donor account or by contacting us directly.
Donations are allocated to the fund selected by the donor wherever possible. Undesignated ("General Donation") contributions are directed to the area of greatest current need, guided by the Trust's annual budget and reviewed by the trustees. All donations above ₹500 receive an automatically generated receipt eligible for 80G tax exemption.
This site uses essential cookies to keep the donation and contact forms working correctly, and limited analytics cookies to understand which pages are most useful to visitors. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.
